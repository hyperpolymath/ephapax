= Ephapax v2 — system architecture and ultracode completion plan

[abstract]
This plan synthesises three candidate routes (proof-first, dogfood-first and seam-contract) and the adversarial critiques of each. The evidence base is a read-only survey of `/home/user/ephapax` and its sibling repositories carried out between 2026-10-27 and 2026-11-04.

The survey ran the following toolchains:

* coqc 8.18.0 on a scratch copy of `formal/`;
* `cargo test` across the ephapax and typed-wasm workspaces;
* Agda 2.6.3 against agda-stdlib v2.3 for the Agda siblings.

Idris2, Lean, Isabelle, zig, julia, z3 and the wasmtime CLI are not installed. Every claim that depends on them is marked *UNVERIFIED*.

Nothing in this document proposes closing or extending `formal/Semantics.v` or `formal/Typing.v`. The legacy `Theorem preservation` is false (`formal/Counterexample.v`). It stayed `Admitted.` by owner directive until the owner ratified D0b (2026-11-05): P0 now retires it to a `Prop` statement, which the owner applies, rather than closing it. Because an `Admitted` statement is an axiom, the legacy theorem together with `Counterexample.v` also derives `False` (`evidence/SurveyCounterexamples.v`, F0). It therefore must never appear in the `Print Assumptions` of any headline theorem; see P0/P1 and D0b.

Throughout, *ephapax-affine* means the internal sublanguage. It is not AffineScript. The two projects share only the compile target, typed-wasm.

== Executive answer

=== Fastest proof route

. *Restore consistency (P0/P1, size S).* Today the build can derive `False`.
** `preservation_l1` (`formal/Semantics_L1.v:3323`, `Admitted.` at :3336) and `step_pop_disjoint_from_type_l1` (:3280, `Admitted.` at :3317) are both refuted by Qed files built in the same `_CoqProject`: `Counterexample_PreservationL1.v:154` and `Counterexample_StepPop.v:145`.
** `env_inconsistent_preservation_l1 : False` and `env_inconsistent_step_pop : False` compile (`evidence/SurveyCounterexamples.v`, F1).
** The owner ratifies retiring both lemmas to `Definition …_stmt : Prop`.
** `preservation_l2_via_l1` and `preservation_l2_lift_case` (`formal/TypingL2.v:334-357`) are made conditional.
** CI must *assert* `Print Assumptions` for every headline theorem against an *empty* allowlist. Today it only prints the output. No refuted `Admitted` may be allowlisted. That includes the legacy `Semantics.preservation`, because with `Counterexample.v` it derives `False` too. Under the ratified D0b (2026-11-05), P0 retires `Semantics.preservation` to `preservation_stmt : Prop`, refuted by `Counterexample.preservation_false`, so no import-closure quarantine is needed (hyperpolymath/ephapax#422).
. *Land the falsifiers in the repo before any design work (P2, size S).* The scratch counterexamples become in-repo Qed regression files against the *current* judgment:
** ClosedCex, EchoCex, EscapeCex, L2Comp, LetCheck;
** PairDup (`has_type_l1` lets a `TProd` variable duplicate a linear `TString` in Linear mode, CLOSED);
** EchoCopy (`TEcho` can be `ECopy`'d in Linear mode).
. *One owner decision packet (P3).* It covers:
** a v2-owned fork of the syntax, typing *and* step relation under `formal/v2/`;
** runtime frames;
** locally nameless region binders;
** latent region effects with prenex region polymorphism;
** per-binder usage grades with the modality as the preorder;
** syntactically distinct echo forms plus `S_Observe`;
** a global function environment for top-level recursion.
. *Build `formal/v2` and test it before proving (P4–P6).*
** Write the skeleton and `Regress.v`, re-encoding every counterexample.
** Write an executable `step_v2_fn` and a naive `check_v2`.
** Write a *typed-by-construction* term generator with a measured acceptance floor, and only then run the property falsifier.
. *Prove L1 with L2 folded in (P7–P10).*
** Prove the opening, renaming and substitution lemmas and the canonical forms.
** Then prove `progress_v2` and `preservation_v2`.
. *Then L3, L4, the meta plane and the certified checker (P11–P15).*

=== Fastest engineering route

. *Close the Rust holes and fail loudly (E0/E1, size S–M).* No owner decision is needed: compound linearity is conservatively rejected, independently of D5 (which may relax it in W1).
** Make `let!` contraction honour `demands_consumption` (`src/ephapax-typing/src/lib.rs:191-201`).
** Reject closures that consume a captured linear value (`lib.rs:1109`).
** Reject pairs that contain a linear value.
** Turn the recursive-`data` stack overflow into an error.
** Make perform/handle a `CodegenError` (`src/ephapax-wasm/src/lib.rs:1991-2000`).
** Make `@total`, `@pure` and `@comptime` errors until they are enforced.
. *One front end and a wasm runner (E3/E4).* `ephapax-driver::load_program` serves every tool, and `ephapax run` executes the compiled wasm through the wasmtime 36 crate.
. *A typed side table from the *existing* checker feeds codegen (E5).* This fixes I64/F32/F64 binops, `String.len` and n-tuples. It is *not* gated on the certified checker.
. *An aLib conformer today (E6).* `stdlib/ALib.eph` plus a wasmtime-based Rust test over pinned vectors. The measured baseline is 89 pass / 1 fail / 13 skip / 51 unimplemented.
. *Then the upstream fix to the typed-wasm access-site convention*, after which the full gate is on by default (E8).
. *Then grammar reconciliation*, generics, `M.Ctor(x)`, recursive types and closures behind `--unstable-core`, the stdlib port, and the LSP.

=== Where the two routes meet

* *The core interchange.* The `ephapax-ir` S-expression codec, plus a new Rust-core→Coq-`SyntaxV2` translator, is the single exchange format for differential testing.
* *The soundness-direction gate.* "Rust accepts ⇒ oracle accepts" is asserted at zero tolerance from the moment `check_v2` exists. The completeness direction is report-only until the Rust checker is rewritten as an image of `CheckV2.v`.
* *One ADR for compound linearity and grades.* The hole exists in *both* Coq (PairDup) and Rust (`pairdup`), so the decision is made once for both.
* *Translation validation at the target.* Every module emitted by the default `compile` path must pass `typed_wasm_gate::gate_module`. Output of `compile --no-verify` is outside this claim, and the flag says so when used. This is how E5a (carrier soundness) is discharged, rather than proving full compiler correctness.

=== Corrections applied to the input routes

* `_CoqProject` does *not* list `Counterexample.v` twice. There is no de-duplication task.
* There are 6 in-repo `Counterexample*.v` files, not 18. Of the other families, 4 are in `evidence/SurveyCounterexamples.v` (outside `_CoqProject`) and 3 were lost and must be re-created; all are landed first (P2).
* LetCheck is a *completeness* gap: v2 must make `let_unit` typable. It is not a term to prove untypable.
* PairDup settles the open X4 question: compound linearity is a hole in the *Coq judgment*, not only in Rust. It therefore moves into the first judgment revision, under the doctrine "holes before anything".
* Editing `TypingL1.v` in place ripples into the fenced `Counterexample.v` (`:186` imports it, and `:192-254` inverts over its constructors). The v2 typing must therefore be forked as well, not only the step relation.
* A configuration-level frame stack alone cannot tell an entered `ERegion` from an unentered one (`Syntax.v:122`). A runtime-only frame form in `SyntaxV2.v` is the main path, not a fallback.
* The EFrame-at-Enter design needs region renaming or opening, and there is none in `formal/`. Latent effects need region polymorphism. Both are added as rungs.
* A falsifier over random terms is vacuous. The typed generator moves into the same wave, with an acceptance floor.
* aLib "≥102 after decimals" is unreachable. With an F64 binding the ceiling is 99 unless string-valued `if_then_else` is also implemented.
* OCaml *is* installed, so extraction is not blocked by the toolchain. Whether OCaml is acceptable for the naive W2 oracle is a policy question (D9a, ratified in W1); the certified-checker form is D9 (W4).
* The Rust↔Coq differential needs a translator that does not exist yet. The Coq core has no recursion and no top-level declarations, so the v2 core gets a global function environment.
* W0 exit gates must not depend on another repository's owner. The typed-wasm gate criteria move to the wave after the upstream fix lands.

=== Components

*Surface and grammar.* There is one normative grammar. The plan's default is to regenerate the EBNF from `ephapax.pest` and back it with a conformance fixture test, keeping every current EBNF production that the parser lacks as UNIMPLEMENTED rather than deleting it. Productions that are not implemented must fail with a named error, and E7's "0 productions unaccounted for" counts them; the specification never shrinks to make that number.

*Desugar.* It stays the only place where sugar lives: `data`/`match` become right-nested sums, records become products, traits become dictionary passing, and contracts become asserts. Each lowering either has a `Desugar.v` lemma or is a pure syntactic macro that has a test.

*Core.* `ephapax-syntax` is a Rust mirror of `SyntaxV2.v`. The additions over today's core are:

* a global function table Σ, giving top-level recursion;
* iso-recursive `TMu` (after D7);
* `TArr` with Rin/Rout;
* region abstraction;
* grades on binders;
* distinct echo forms;
* `EObserve`.

`EFrame` is runtime-only: the parser cannot produce it, and only `Enter` creates it.

*Checker (L1–L4).* `ephapax-check-v2` is an input/output-context algorithm in the Hodas–Miller style. It has one function per `has_type_v2` constructor, named after that constructor. It returns `(Ty, R', G')` and emits the typed core.

`ephapax-typing` remains as a façade until switch-over. `ephapax-linear` either becomes a thin linter over the grades or is deprecated, according to the D5 ADR.

*Meta plane.* `@comptime` is a halting island:

* no free recursive `comptime_call`;
* built-in bounded folds over finite type descriptors, with a cut-off at recursive types;
* no comptime inside `@emit_decl`;
* spliced output is re-checked by S3.

`ephapax-meta`'s reflective `quote`/`eval` is fenced to that island. This needs a decision against ADR-016.

*IR.* The typed core is the IR. `ephapax-ir` is the exchange format between the driver and the oracle; it is not a new optimisation layer.

*typed-wasm backend.* Linear memory with a bump allocator and region stack, which is today's design. On top of that:

* typed instruction selection;
* a funcref table using the ADR-005 closure ABI `[fnId@0, envPtr@4]`;
* carriers as listed in the diagram;
* `ret_kind` populated;
* an Affine ownership kind and a module mode record, once typed-wasm ratifies them.

The *interop representation* question (linear-memory layout profile vs WasmGC) is D10.

*Runtime.* There is one embedded runtime, generated by `ephapax-wasm`. `ephapax-runtime` and `ephapax-analysis` are either wired in or explicitly retired. Scrub-on-exit (`memory.fill` over the freed bump range) applies to regions marked sensitive.

*stdlib via aLib.* `stdlib/ALib.eph` is scored against vendored, pinned aLib vectors. `Aggregate.eph` follows the echo-types `EchoAggregation` monoid-homomorphism shape. `Transactions.eph` and `Reversible.eph` are RMR; `Erasure.eph` is RMO.

*Tooling.* LSP, REPL, query, fmt/lint and the package manager all run on the driver. The package manager's lockfile tier feeds the import resolver.

=== Detours and optional rungs

These are kept off the critical path.

* *P20 graded-effect layer* (cost = MaxPlus, occupancy = HWM): a Coq port of resource-algebra Core. It is orthogonal to L2. Size L.
* *P21 `erase_noninterference`* (RMO): a binary logical relation with an `is-OND` observation model. Physical scrub is a *tagged METAL-BOUNDARY axiom*. That needs explicit owner sign-off, must be allowlisted by name, and must never appear in the `Print Assumptions` of any L1/L2 theorem. Size XL.
* *IFC layer* over secret-types TINI. Size XL; deferred.
* *Generalising L2 into an anytype-style single algebra.* This is a detour. No existing interface hosts usage grades, cost, HWM and labels together (idempotent ⊕ vs 1+1=ω), and doing so would break four-layer orthogonality.
* *Full E5b* semantic compiler correctness. XL; deferred behind translation validation.
* *Explicitly out of scope:* anything touching `Semantics.v`, `Typing.v`, `Syntax.v` or the legacy preservation statement, except the owner-ratified D0/D0b retirement under P0/T0g (an agent prepares it; the owner reviews and signs it).

=== Theory hooks that could give a more final solution

* *Semantic soundness* via a unary step-indexed or Iris-style logical relation with region liveness as a world or resource. It gives safety without syntactic preservation. The *same* relation, made binary, delivers `erase_noninterference` (P21) and an IFC result. This is the fallback if K1 fires (section 10), and arguably the more final architecture once L1 is stable.
* *Proof-producing checker.* `ephapax-check-v2` emits a typing derivation, and Coq checks it, or an extracted certificate checker does. This removes Rust from the trusted base per program, without extracting the whole checker (D9 option D).
* *RichWasm* (Coq-mechanised typed Wasm with linear/unrestricted qualifiers and regions) as a target-IR model. It turns E5 into a typed translation.
* *QTT grades with a mode preorder.* Linear vs Affine is exactly whether `1 ≤ 0` holds. This unifies L2 placement (D5), the Rust `let`/`let!` split and the wire ownership kinds.
* *Adjoint/LNL shifts* for L4 (Pruiksma–Pfenning mode preorders).
* *Calcagno–Helsen–Thiemann* syntactic region soundness (runtime frames). *Tofte–Talpin* latent effects and region polymorphism. *Aydemir et al.* cofinite quantification for region binders.
* *2LTT / staged calculi* for the meta plane: a total meta level generating a partial object level.
* *Abstract interpretation over Wasm locals* (must/may use-count lattice) for the typed-wasm L10 fix.

=== Ratified: proof classification (owner, 2026-11-09)

Ephapax's soundness statements are *theory* under PROOF-POLICY §1: the typing judgment and step relation as a calculus, progress, preservation, the layer obligations (L1–L4) and the refutations of the retired statements. Each gets an Agda source of truth.

* *Grades.* Every such result is `ungraded` until its Agda proof checks under `agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible` (`PWG-3`). A grade is derived from receipts, never asserted (§8.4).
* *Coq becomes a recheck leg.* The existing Coq proofs, and the Coq P-rungs of this plan, are the Rocq leg of §2. A Coq proof counts towards `PWG-2i`/`PWG-2ii` only once the matching Agda statement exists and the correspondence is `RECONCILED` (§2.2). Until then it is target-only evidence (§3). The Lean leg is transported through Dedukti where the pinned exporter allows it, and otherwise re-proved by hand without Mathlib.
* *First targets.* The refutations are small and closed (`preservation_false`, `step_pop_disjoint_from_type_l1_FALSE`, `preservation_l1_FALSE`). Porting them needs only the fragment of the legacy and L1 judgments their witnesses touch. They are the first Agda rows, and they test the correspondence method before the v2 core is ported.
* *What stays engineering.* The Idris2 ABI, the Rust checker and code generator (Creusot/Pearlite and Verus, #378) and typed-wasm emission are about this repository's implementation. They keep the §1 engineering mapping and are not graded.
* *Plan impact.* The P-ladder is written for Coq, because the executable oracle (D9a, D9) extracts from Coq. This ruling adds an Agda port obligation to every headline rung. The order of the two is a new decision, *D13*, in the W0 packet: Coq first then Agda port, Agda first with Coq as the recheck leg, or both developed together. Under the decision-branch rule, no P-rung past P4 starts until D13 is ratified with its branch table.
* *Beyond this repo.* The PWG ledger lives in `PROOF-NEEDS.adoc`. Mathesis may catalogue the grades it derives from those receipts (PROOF-POLICY §8.5). The memory entry `feedback_ephapax_no_patching_legacy_preservation.md` should record the classification.

=== D7 — Recursive types

*Ratified 2026-11-10 (owner):* the recommended option, iso-recursive `TMu` with fold/unfold inserted by desugar. Its first use is rung E9 in the Rust implementation (gate `src/ephapax-cli/tests/recursive_types.rs`), behind `--unstable-core`. `data` types that refer to each other become nested `TMu` types (Bekic's construction, in the order of their names), so the core has no new former. A dense cycle makes these types grow fast (4 types that each refer to each of the 4 need 3613 bodies, 5 need 8 million), so a type of more than 10000 bodies, or of more than 256 nested named types, is a named error. A recursive type of n variables (as the `rec` groups of WasmGC) removes that limit and is a change of the core. A reference with other type arguments is a named error.

=== D14 — A type ascription in the core (added 2026-11-10)

A constructor of a `data` type that is not recursive left no core node that carries the declared type: `MkBox(true)` passed for `data Box = MkBox(I32)` until a use of the value as a `Box`. A constructor of a recursive type has the check from its `fold` (D7). This decision is the core form that carries the check for the other `data` types.

*Interim (owner, 2026-11-10):* the desugarer emits `let #C: T = v in #C`. `T` is the declared type and binds the type parameters of the `data` type. The calculus has this term already (`ELet v (EVar 0)`; its `let` has no annotation), so no proof file changes. Gate: `a_constructor_checks_its_arguments_against_the_declaration` in `src/ephapax-cli/tests/run_wasm.rs`. The decision stays open until the owner decides the nominal or the bidirectional design. It is taken with the P4 core, where `TMu`, `fold` and `unfold` enter the calculus, so that the core is extended 1 time.

An ascription `EAscribe T e` needs these parts in the P4 core:

* 1 expression former, and 1 case in each function over expressions;
* 2 step rules: `EAscribe T v` steps to `v`, and the congruence rule. The form is not a value, so each canonical forms lemma keeps 1 case for each type;
* 1 typing rule with 1 premise, which passes the region environment and the context through. It has no sibling, so it needs no side condition;
* 1 case in each proof by induction on an expression, a step or a typing derivation;
* the instantiated type as the annotation, because the calculus has no type variables (`inl` and `inr` have this gap already);
* the same former in the Agda source (D13) and in the Idris2 copy of the IR.

[cols="3,1,1,4",options="header"]
|===
|Option |(a) |(b) |Comment

|*Recommended:* an ascription former in the P4 core, added with `TMu`, `fold` and `unfold`
|4 |4
|The standard switch from type synthesis to type checking. It can later make the annotations of `inl` and `inr` unnecessary. It does not make `Box` different from `I32`.

|Keep the annotated `let`
|3 |5
|No new former and no proof work. Each constructor costs 1 administrative step and 1 wasm local, and the name of the binder carries the name of the constructor.

|An ascription in the Rust core only, removed before the calculus
|3 |4
|No extra step or local. The calculus does not change, and the Coq to Rust bridge gets 1 more erased form.

|Unconventional: a nominal constructor form (each `data` type is a named type)
|4 |4
|Also closes the case that this decision leaves open: a value that no constructor made passes as the `data` type (`f(5)` for `fn f(b: Box)`). It is the nominal option of D7, which was not ratified, so it needs the branch table of D7.
